A place to return to the wisdom of the Church. Search quoted words of saints and Catholic writers alongside cited passages from primary texts—then follow each record back to its source.

What this archive does

Find a passageSearch by a name, a work, or a word such as mercy, prayer, grace, or truth.

Keep the contextEvery result retains its author, citation, source link, and licence note wherever that information is available.

Read with careA study and prayer aid, not a substitute for the Church’s official texts, sound catechesis, or spiritual direction.

The archive

Browse attributed quotations and cited primary texts. “Primary text” means an excerpt from a work rather than a short standalone saying.
